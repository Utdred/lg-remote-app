Connect to your LG TV

  1. Make sure your iPhone and TV are on the same Wi‑Fi.
  2. Find the TV's IP address: on the TV go to Settings → Network → Wi‑Fi (or Wired) → Advanced settings.
  3. Turn on Settings → General → Devices → External Devices → TV On With Mobile (wording varies by model). Some models also need LG Connect Apps on.
  1. One-time step: open the TV's secure address in Safari. Tap Show Details → visit this website and confirm. A blank or error page afterwards is normal. Then come back here.
  2. Tap Connect, then press Yes / Accept on the TV with the physical remote.
Troubleshooting

If it won't connect: repeat step 4 (Safari can forget the exception), check the IP, and make sure the TV is on. iOS may ask to allow access to devices on your local network; allow it. Turning the TV on isn't possible from a web page, only off.
